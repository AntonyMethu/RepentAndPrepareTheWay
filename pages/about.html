Ministry of Repentance & Holiness

Our Mission

The Ministry of Repentance and Holiness has one central, unwavering mission: to prepare the Church of Jesus Christ for His Glorious Second Coming.

Founded in Nakuru, Kenya, this Ministry has been calling all people — across every nation, every denomination, every background — to repent, to walk in genuine holiness, and to make themselves ready for the return of the King of kings.

The message has never changed. It is the same message that John the Baptist preached in the wilderness, the same message of Isaiah 40:3 — "Prepare the way for the LORD; make straight in the desert a highway for our GOD."

The Ministry's Work

📢

Evangelism & Crusades

Massive open-air crusades held across Kenya and Africa, drawing hundreds of thousands of people to hear the Word of GOD.

📻

Radio Broadcasting

Jesus is LORD Radio broadcasts 24/7 from Nakuru, reaching listeners worldwide with the message of repentance and holiness.

🔮

Prophetic Ministry

Over 150 documented fulfilled prophecies — earthquakes, wars, diseases, and political events all foretold before they happened.

✨

Signs & Wonders

GOD confirms His Word through miracles — the blind see, cripples walk, HIV/AIDS is healed, and the dead are raised.
