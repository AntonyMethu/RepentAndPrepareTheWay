🙏 Salvation Prayer

Give Your Life to Jesus Christ

The Messiah is coming. Now — today — is the day of salvation. GOD loves you and desires that you be part of His glorious Bride.

"For GOD so loved the world that he gave his one and only Son, that whoever believes in him shall not perish but have eternal life."

— John 3:16

GOD loves you. He created you for a purpose, and He desires that you know Him personally — not as a distant force, but as a loving Father.

But sin has separated us from GOD. Every person who has ever lived has sinned and fallen short of the glory of GOD (Romans 3:23). The consequence of sin is death — eternal separation from the One who made us.

But GOD, in His great love and mercy, provided a way. He sent His only Son, Jesus Christ, to take the punishment for our sin. Jesus died on the cross, bearing every sin you have ever committed. He was buried. And on the third day, He rose from the dead — conquering sin and death forever.

Now He offers you the free gift of salvation. Not because you deserve it. Not because you are good enough. But because of His grace.

If you are ready to receive this gift, pray the prayer below — sincerely, from your heart. GOD hears every prayer, and He is faithful to receive all who come to Him.

Pray This Prayer

"Heavenly Father, I come before You today acknowledging that I am a sinner. I have fallen short of Your glory and I deserve the consequences of my sin. But I believe that Your Son, Jesus Christ, died on the cross for my sins, was buried, and rose again on the third day.

LORD Jesus, I ask You to forgive me of all my sins — every thought, word, and deed that has been contrary to Your holy standard. I turn away from my sin right now. I repent with my whole heart.

I confess with my mouth that Jesus Christ is LORD, and I believe in my heart that GOD raised Him from the dead. I receive You now as my personal LORD and Saviour.

Come into my heart, LORD Jesus. Be the LORD of my life — my thoughts, my words, my relationships, my future. Fill me with Your Holy Spirit and lead me in Your ways.

I thank You, Father, that I am now Your child. I thank You for the gift of eternal life. Help me to walk in repentance and holiness, to prepare for the day when You return in glory.

In the precious Name of Jesus Christ, I pray. Amen."

"If you declare with your mouth, 'Jesus is Lord,' and believe in your heart that GOD raised him from the dead, you will be saved. For it is with your heart that you believe and are justified, and it is with your mouth that you profess your faith and are saved."

— Romans 10:9–10

What Happens Next?

Read the Bible Daily

Begin reading GOD's Word every day. Start with the Gospel of John, then Romans. The Bible is your food for the new life in Christ.

Pray Every Day

Talk to GOD — He hears you. Prayer is your conversation with your Heavenly Father. Come to Him in the morning, throughout the day, and at night.

Find a Church That Preaches Holiness

Connect with a local church that preaches genuine repentance, holiness, and the return of Christ. You need fellowship with other believers.

Walk in Repentance Daily

Salvation is not a one-time event — it is a daily walk. Examine your heart. Confess and turn from sin daily. GOD is faithful to cleanse you (1 John 1:9).

Prepare for the Return of Jesus Christ

The Messiah is coming soon. Read the prophecies, study the signs of the times, and live with your heart ready. He is coming for a holy and prepared Bride.
